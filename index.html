01 — About

A tutor who has stood at the bedside first

Gargi Shukla is a dedicated and compassionate nursing professional with clinical and teaching experience. She worked as a Nursing Tutor cum Clinical Instructor at Krishna Nursing and Paramedical Institute, Lucknow, from 2023 to 2025, with 1 year and 10 months of teaching and clinical instructional experience, gaining valuable experience in nursing education, clinical instruction, and student mentoring.

Currently, she is pursuing her M.Sc. Nursing (2025 Batch) in Community Health Nursing from Vivekananda College of Nursing, Lucknow.

Her professional interests include Nursing Education, Clinical Skill Development, Student Mentoring, Community Health Nursing, Patient Safety, Nursing Research, and Professional Development.

She aims to contribute to nursing education by integrating her clinical experience, teaching skills, and academic knowledge to promote clinical competence and academic excellence among nursing students.
